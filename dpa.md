# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between the customer subscribing to the LodgeTrack services ("Data Controller") and Veljko Galović, an independent developer residing in Sombor, Serbia, operating and providing the multi-tenant property management platform under the trade name "LodgeTrack" ("Data Processor").

## 1. Definitions
1.1 "Personal Data", "Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject", and "Processing" shall have the meanings given in applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection.
1.2 "Services" means the multi-tenant property management SaaS platform provided via [https://www.lodgetrack.com](https://www.lodgetrack.com).
1.3 "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to assist in fulfilling its obligations under this Agreement.

## 2. Scope and Details of Processing
2.1 **Categories of Data Subjects:** Tenants, prospective tenants, property owners, guests, and Controller’s administrative users.
2.2 **Categories of Personal Data:** Names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, payment/billing references, lease terms, and usage/authentication logs.
2.3 **Nature and Purpose of Processing:** Processing is performed solely to provide, secure, maintain, and support the LodgeTrack multi-tenant property management services in accordance with the Terms of Service.

## 3. Obligations of the Processor
3.1 The Processor shall:
* **a)** Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country.
* **b)** Ensure that persons authorized to process the Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality.
* **c)** Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a security level appropriate to the risk, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, and logical data isolation between tenants.
* **d)** Not engage any Sub-processor other than those pre-authorized in accordance with Section 4.
* **e)** Assist the Controller, by appropriate technical and organizational measures, in responding to Data Subject rights requests.
* **f)** Notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data breach affecting Controller data.
* **g)** At the choice of the Controller, delete or return all Personal Data to the Controller upon termination of the Services.

## 4. Sub-processors
4.1 The Controller authorizes the Processor to engage the following infrastructure Sub-processors to deliver the Services:
* Cloudflare, Inc. (Hosting, CDN, and Edge Infrastructure)
* Supabase, Inc. (Database and File Storage Infrastructure)
* Auth.js / Authentication providers (Authentication services)
4.2 The Processor shall impose data protection obligations on Sub-processors that offer at least the same level of protection as set out in this Agreement.

## 5. International Transfers
5.1 Personal Data processed under this Agreement may be hosted or accessed via infrastructure providers operating globally. Where data is transferred outside its country of origin, the Processor ensures appropriate legal safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent adequacy frameworks) are in place.

## 6. Audits
6.1 The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and allow for audits conducted by the Controller or an auditor mandated by the Controller.

## 7. Governing Law
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Serbia.
