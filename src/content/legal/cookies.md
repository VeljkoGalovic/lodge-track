# Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and improve your experience. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device for a set period).

## 2. How We Use Cookies

LodgeTrack uses cookies for the following purposes:

### Strictly Necessary Cookies (Always Active)
These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `next-auth.session-token` / `__Secure-next-auth.session-token` | Keeps you signed in across sessions (NextAuth.js) | 30 days |
| `next-auth.callback-url` | Handles OAuth callback redirects | Session |
| `next-auth.csrf-token` | CSRF protection for authentication forms | Session |
| `locale` | Remembers your language preference | 1 year |
| `theme` | Remembers your light/dark/system theme preference | 1 year |
| `cookie-consent` | Stores your cookie consent preferences | 1 year |

### Analytics Cookies (Optional)
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We currently do **not** use third-party analytics cookies, but if we enable them in the future (e.g., for product improvement), they would fall into this category and require your consent.

### Marketing Cookies (Optional)
These cookies are used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements. We do **not** use marketing or advertising cookies.

## 3. Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer or by visiting the Cookie Settings page. Your preferences are stored in a `cookie-consent` cookie that lasts for 1 year.

### Changing Your Preferences
- **Accept All**: Allows all categories of cookies
- **Reject Non-Essential**: Only allows strictly necessary cookies
- **Customize**: Choose which optional categories to enable

### Browser Controls
You can also control cookies through your browser settings:
- **Chrome**: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
- **Edge**: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Note: Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break core functionality like signing in.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not currently use any third-party cookies. If we integrate third-party services in the future (e.g., analytics providers, payment processors), we will update this policy and request your consent before setting any non-essential third-party cookies.

## 5. Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit LodgeTrack, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:
- Accept all cookies
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Customize your preferences per category

Your choice is saved and the banner will not reappear for 1 year unless you clear your browser data or visit from a new device/browser.
